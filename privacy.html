Privacy Policy

cjaviationtn.org — the internal operations site of C&J Aviation LLC, Maryville, Tennessee. Last updated September 22, 2026.

What this site is

cjaviationtn.org is a private, internal tool used by the owners and employees of C&J Aviation LLC to run the business: bookkeeping, purchase orders, bench stock inventory, tool calibration records, and the employee time clock. It is not offered to the public and has no customers or visitors other than the company's own people.

Signing in with Google

The site uses Sign in with Google only to confirm who you are. From your Google account it receives your email address, your name, and your profile picture. Your email address is matched against the company's list of owners and employees to decide what you may see; the name and picture are shown on screen and nowhere else. The site does not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts, or anything else in your Google account, and does not ask for permission to.

What the site stores

Business records live in the company's own Google Sheets, owned by C&J Aviation LLC. For employees, that includes the work orders you clock in and out on, the hours and pay that result, and the bench stock parts you take or restock. A short-lived sign-in session token is kept in your browser so you don't have to sign in on every visit; it expires on its own after 30 days and is deleted when you sign out or when your access is removed.

Sharing

Nothing collected by this site is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party. Payroll data is used only to pay you and to keep the company's books.

Your access

Access is granted and removed by the company's owners. When an employee leaves, their sign-in is disabled and any open session is ended. Questions about this policy or about your data go to the company at cjaviationtn@gmail.com.
